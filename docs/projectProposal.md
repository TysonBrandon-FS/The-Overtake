# The Overtake

## Application Definition Statement

The Overtake is a WordPress based eCommerce website for racing inspired custom merchandise. The site will let users browse products, view merch collections, add items to their cart, and complete checkout online. The project is being built with WordPress, Elementor, WooCommerce, Printify, and WooPayments.

## Target Market

The target market is racing fans who enjoy motorsport culture and want custom merch with a clean, modern, high energy style. This includes Formula 1 fans, casual racing fans, car culture fans, and people who like bold sports themed clothing or wall art.

## Primary Research

For this project, I looked at the kind of merch racing fans usually buy, such as shirts, hoodies, posters, wall art, mouse pads, and accessories. I also looked at how eCommerce sites organize products and how customers expect a shop page, cart, and checkout to work.

## Secondary Research

I researched WordPress, Elementor, WooCommerce, Printify, and WooPayments to figure out the best setup for building the site. WordPress and Elementor are being used for the website and page design. WooCommerce is being used for the store. Printify is being used for print on demand product fulfillment. WooPayments is being used for checkout and payment processing.

## Use Cases

### Use Case 1

A user visits The Overtake and browses the shop page. They look through available products, filter or sort items, and choose a product they like.

### Use Case 2

A user opens a product page, reviews the product details, selects product options if available, and adds the item to the cart.

### Use Case 3

A user goes to checkout, enters their information, chooses a payment method, and places an order through WooCommerce.

### Use Case 4

After an order is placed, the product information connects to Printify so the item can be produced and shipped through the print provider workflow.

## Problem Statement

Racing fans have a lot of interest in merch, but many merch stores feel generic or do not have a strong visual style. Some stores also make it hard to browse products clearly or understand the checkout process. The Overtake is meant to create a more polished racing inspired shopping experience.

## Pain Points

1. Some racing merch feels too basic or generic

2. Product pages can feel cluttered or hard to use

3. Store navigation is not always clear

4. Checkout needs to feel simple and trustworthy

5. Print on demand setup needs to be organized so products can be fulfilled correctly

## Solution Statement

The Overtake solves this by creating a clean WordPress eCommerce store with a strong racing inspired design style. Elementor will be used to build the front end layout, WooCommerce will manage the products and checkout, Printify will handle print on demand fulfillment, and WooPayments will support the payment process.

## Competition

Direct competition includes motorsport merch stores, custom racing apparel shops, and print on demand stores that sell car or racing themed products.

Indirect competition includes general clothing stores, sports merch shops, and marketplaces like Etsy where people sell custom fan inspired products.

The Overtake is different because it is focused on combining racing inspired design, custom merch, and a polished eCommerce experience in one site.

## Features and Functionality

1. **Homepage**
   Introduces the brand and guides users toward the shop.

2. **Shop Page**
   Displays the available products in a clean product grid.

3. **Product Pages**
   Shows product images, prices, descriptions, and product options.

4. **Cart**
   Lets users review selected products before checkout.

5. **Checkout**
   Allows users to enter their information and complete payment.

6. **WooCommerce Products**
   Manages product listings, pricing, variations, and store data.

7. **Printify Integration**
   Supports product creation, mockups, fulfillment, and shipping.

8. **Payment Integration**
   Uses WooPayments for checkout and test payments.

9. **Elementor Page Design**
   Builds the front end layouts and visual style of the site.

10. **Project Tracking**
    Uses GitHub, screenshots, exports, notes, and activity logs to track progress for the assignment.

## Integrations

1. **WordPress**
   Main website platform and content management system.

2. **Elementor**
   Visual page builder for layouts, templates, and front end design.

3. **WooCommerce**
   Store system for products, cart, checkout, and eCommerce settings.

4. **Printify**
   Print provider for product mockups, production, fulfillment, and shipping.

5. **WooPayments**
   Payment system for checkout and test payments.

6. **GitHub**
   Tracks project files, documentation, screenshots, exports, and custom code.

7. **Activity Log Plugin**
   Tracks WordPress admin actions that GitHub cannot automatically see.

## Final Goal

The final goal is to have a working WordPress eCommerce website that looks polished, supports product browsing and checkout, connects to Printify for merch fulfillment, and is documented clearly through GitHub for instructor review.
